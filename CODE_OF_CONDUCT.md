# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make the BookmarkBridge community welcoming, safe, and equitable for everyone.

We respect the dignity, rights, and contributions of every participant, regardless of race, ethnicity, caste, color, age, physical characteristics, neurodiversity, disability, sex or gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, language, philosophy or religion, national or social origin, socioeconomic position, education, or other status.

Everyone participating in good faith and in accordance with this Covenant receives the same privileges of participation.

## Encouraged behavior

Words and actions can be interpreted differently across cultures, backgrounds, and languages. With that in mind, behavior that sustains this community includes:

- Respecting the purpose of the project and its community spaces.
- Engaging kindly and honestly with other people.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Taking responsibility for actions and contributions.
- Gracefully giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Committing to repair harm when it occurs.
- Acting in other ways that promote the community's well-being.

## Restricted behavior

Instances, threats, or promotion of the following behaviors violate this Code of Conduct:

- Harassment, including violating an expressed boundary or continuing unwanted personal attention after a clear request to stop.
- Character attacks, including insulting, demeaning, or pejorative comments aimed at a person or group.
- Stereotyping or discrimination based on identity or traits.
- Sexualization or inappropriately intimate behavior in project spaces.
- Violating confidentiality or sharing private information without permission.
- Causing, encouraging, or threatening violence or other harm.
- Other conduct that threatens the well-being of the community.

### Other restrictions

- Do not impersonate another person or misrepresent an identity to evade enforcement.
- Credit the sources of contributed content.
- Do not post promotional or commercial material outside the community's norms.
- Present material involving restricted behavior responsibly and with appropriate context.

## Reporting an issue

Not every disagreement is a Code of Conduct violation. Participants should first use the encouraged behaviors above when a safe, direct resolution is possible.

Report possible violations confidentially through GitHub's private reporting controls. If private vulnerability reporting is enabled, use **Security → Advisories → Report a vulnerability** and identify the submission clearly as a Code of Conduct report.

Do not publish a sensitive conduct report in an issue. Project maintainers will review available messages, logs, and witness accounts as needed. They will prioritize safety and confidentiality, keep enforcement private with the people involved, and communicate more broadly only when a safe resolution calls for it.

## Addressing and repairing harm

Project maintainers will consider the incident's impact on individuals and the community. Depending on severity and history, they may skip lower steps in this enforcement ladder:

1. **Warning.** A private written warning for an incident or related series of incidents. Repair may include acknowledging responsibility, apologizing, and clarifying expectations.
2. **Temporarily limited activities.** A time-limited restriction following a repeated violation or a more serious first violation. Repair may include reflection, an apology, and a careful return to community spaces.
3. **Temporary suspension.** Removal from project spaces for a defined period, with written conditions for return. Repair includes respecting both the terms and spirit of the suspension.
4. **Permanent ban.** Removal from all project spaces when repeated measures have not resolved the behavior or a violation is too serious for safe continued participation.

This ladder is a guideline. Maintainers may use their judgment to protect the community and support meaningful repair.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces and when someone officially represents BookmarkBridge in public or private spaces.
